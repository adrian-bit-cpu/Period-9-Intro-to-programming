My Dream Trip To Mexico

Ultimate Travel Guide

Trip Research

Mexico is a beautiful country with a rich culture and diverse landscapes. The food there is also on point! If you ever wanted to visit mexico, I reccomend going to Visit Mexico. Another good site to check out is TripAdvisor. Lastly, to ensure that you have the best experience possible, make sure to research the local weather of where you want to go. You can do that at Weather.com.

Getting There & Staying There

Flight & Hotel Booking

Finding a good flight and booking a good hotel is the hardest part of planning for a vacation. Sometimes a flight is too late for you to make an event you planned, or a flight is too early and you oversleep. A hotel you book can also be too expensive, or not quality for what you deserve. You must not fret, because with the use of these resources, you can find the best deals and have a great time! For your flights, I would use Expedia. For your hotel, I would use Booking.com.

Transportation Info

Once you're in Mexico, you can use various forms of transportation such as taxis, buses, or rental cars. It's a good idea to research the transportation options in the area you plan to visit. You should be aware of where you are going and the best ways to get there. I would use Google Maps to help navigate. To find or purchase transportation, I would recommend using Uber or Lyft.
