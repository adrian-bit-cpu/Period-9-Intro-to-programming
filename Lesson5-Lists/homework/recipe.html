Mongolian Beef Noodles

The reason why I chose Mongolian beef noodles was because recently I have made this recipe and it was REALLY good. It was sweet and savory. It was also really easy to make. it took me 20-30 minutes and it was so worth it. It was very protein packed, and low fat.

Ingredients

  1. 8 oz Linguine noodles
  2. 1 1/2 lbs Ground beef (93% lean or more)
  3. Salt and Pepper
  4. 6 cloves of garlic minced
  5. 4 tsps of grated ginger
  6. 4 green onions sliced
  7. 3 tbsps brown sugar
  8. 1/2 cups Beef broth (low sodium)
  9. 3 tbsps Hoisin sauce
  10. 1/2 cups soy sauce (low sodium)
  11. cornstarch + 1/4 cup water
  12. 1/4 tsps red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles
    • Cook spaghetti in boiling water.
    • When al dente, drain and set aside.
  2. Cook the beef
    • Add ground beef to a hot pan.
    • Break and flatten until browned.
    • Keep stirring until fully cooked.
  3. Add garlic
    • Chop/mince garlic.
    • Add to cooked beef and stir together.
  4. Season the beef
    • Add all the seasonings listed above.(Salt and pepper, ginger, brown sugar.)
    • Stir the beef and seasonings so its evenly coated.
  5. Make the sauce
    • Add the dark sauces listed.(Soy sauce, Hoisin sauce.)
    • Add additional liquids listed.(Beef broth, cornstarch slurry.)
    • Stir it all together, let the sauce simmer.
  6. Add the noodles
    • Add the cooked spaghetti to the pot with the beef sauce.
    • Toss/stir until all noodles are completely coated.
  7. Serve
    • Transfer to a bowl.
    • Top with the green onions.
    • Enjoy!

Notes

  1. Noodles
    • Any form of noodes similar to linguini would work. Do not worry if you don't have linguini.
    • If you didn't know, al dente means close to cooked, as in almost fully cooked.
  2. Beef
    • You do not need any butter or anything in the pot with the beef when it is cooking.
    • Add a little water with the beef while it cooks.
